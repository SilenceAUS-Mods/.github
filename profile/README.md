**SilenceAUS Mods**

Arma Reforger mods by Silence, built for the Arma Reforger community and the ARGC servers.

Mods are published on the Arma Reforger Workshop. Source repositories here are private.

## AI

| Mod | What it does |
|---|---|
| [SilenceAI - Enhanced Combat](https://reforger.armaplatform.com/workshop/6A1AA2A95D40BC57) | Makes Reforger's AI fight like trained infantry instead of a crowd of individual riflemen. |
| Silence AI Helicopters | AI helicopter behaviour for the ARGC servers. |
| [Silence Groups - no RHS](https://reforger.armaplatform.com/workshop/6A6053E82C60BC58) | No-RHS replacement for the ARGC Groups mod. *(WIP)* |
